In the information age, continuous learning is the only skill that won't become obsolete. Entrepreneurs must constantly feed their minds with new ideas to find breakthroughs and stay successful.

Brian Tracy highlights two powerful methods for efficient learning: turning drive time into a "university on wheels" with audio programs and attending seminars to gain condensed knowledge.

These strategies allow individuals to acquire the equivalent of advanced degrees and critical business insights without traditional schooling, ultimately leading to greater success and higher earnings.

Key takeaways

  • Continuous learning is essential: According to Peter Drucker, the ability to learn and apply new skills is the only ability that won't become obsolete in the 21st century. All other skills will eventually become outdated.
  • Entrepreneurs must constantly learn: Successful business people are those who continually feed their minds with new ideas, as any single idea could be a breakthrough for their business.
  • Leverage audio programs: Driving for 500 to 1,000 hours a year is equivalent to three to six months of full-time university attendance. Listening to educational audio programs during this time can provide the equivalent of a doctoral education annually, offering immediately applicable business knowledge.
  • Attend seminars: A good business seminar offers thousands of hours of research and experience condensed into a few hours. Attending these can save hundreds of thousands of dollars and countless hours of self-study.
  • Focus on application: The goal is to build a business, sell products, and make a profit. Efficiently acquiring knowledge through audio learning and seminars allows you to become highly knowledgeable and successful without needing to read hundreds of books yourself.
