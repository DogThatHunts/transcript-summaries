The 3 Things People Always Pay For | Business & Marketing Psychology

Why we shell out cash for cures, shortcuts, and unforgettable vibes—and how to turn that insight into a winning niche.

Summary

Humans only spend money on three things: a solution that removes a problem, convenience that saves effort, and an experience that delivers a unique feeling. The speaker argues that people will pay almost anything to eliminate pain, stress, debt, or loneliness, and that the richest companies profit by making life easier. Choosing the one of these three you can excel at will set you apart in business.

Key takeaways

  • People pay for solutions that make problems disappear, such as hiring a doctor to relieve pain or stress.
  • Convenience commands a premium; the most successful companies simplify tasks, making them faster and less of a headache.
  • Experiences sell feelings beyond the product itself, exemplified by Starbucks charging extra for the ambiance, not just the coffee.
  • The advice is to focus on the one category you can dominate—solution, convenience, or experience—and let the others follow.
