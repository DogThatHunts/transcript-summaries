He Cracked Every Layer of Reality… And Became a Target

Macrobiotic philosopher Michio Kushi deconstructs the illusions of physical matter, ownership, and modern societal structures, tracing all of existence back to a single, vibrating cosmic source.

In this lecture, macrobiotic educator and philosopher Michio Kushi outlines a holistic cosmology that traces human existence back to a single, infinite source. He argues that the physical world, human consciousness, and societal structures are often misunderstood due to sensory illusions, proposing instead that everything is connected through a continuous spectrum of vibration.

The evolutionary chain of reality

Kushi describes a multi-stage descent from the absolute to the relative world. In his view, reality begins with "one infinity" (the non-phenomenal spirit or God), which polarizes into two primary, complementary forces: yin and yang (representing expansion and contraction). The interaction of these forces generates vibration and energy, which then condense into pre-atomic particles, physical elements, the plant kingdom, and ultimately, human beings. Because humans are the culmination of this billions-of-years journey, Kushi asserts that we carry the memory of this shared cosmic origin, making all of humanity fundamentally interconnected.

Matter as vibration and the power of thought

Drawing parallels to modern physics, Kushi argues that solid matter is an illusion. Because atoms are mostly empty space, he describes physical forms as "ghosts" appearing and disappearing within invisible fields of energy. He extends this vibrational model to human consciousness, asserting that thoughts are literal vibrations dispatched into the environment.

During a live demonstration, Kushi illustrates how shifting mental focus—from peaceful landscapes to war—alters a student's energy. He compares the mind to a blue sky and thoughts to clouds; deep meditation allows one to connect back to the cloudless "absolute world," while dwelling on worry, anger, or fear creates a self-imposed, illusionary reality.

The illusion of ownership and the creation of enemies

Kushi challenges the foundational concepts of modern society, beginning with ownership. He argues that property, money, and even nations are mental constructs rather than absolute realities. Because these assets ultimately return to nature or change hands, humans only "use" them temporarily.

This same "egocentric thinking," Kushi claims, leads to the creation of external enemies. He argues that sickness, viruses, and bacteria are not hostile invaders, but rather indicators of internal disharmony and self-abuse. In Kushi's view, a healthy body naturally resists disease, whereas an unhealthy lifestyle makes the body a "paradise" for pathogens or pests. He suggests that blaming external forces—such as the devil or genetic inheritance—is an arrogant refusal to acknowledge our own lifestyle choices.

The modern delusional system of life

Kushi critiques the standard trajectory of modern life—moving from school to a corporate career, and finally to retirement at age 65—as a "delusional system." He argues that this structure reduces the grand cosmic journey of human life to a commodity, where individuals sell their time for money. He points to the persistence of degenerative diseases, mental illness, and widespread unhappiness as evidence that this societal blueprint is fundamentally out of harmony with the natural order.

Waking up through gratitude and macrobiotics

To escape this modern "nightmare," Kushi advocates for a conscious awakening rooted in gratitude and dietary alignment. He argues that true happiness begins with expressing appreciation for ancestors, nature, and food.

Crucially, Kushi asserts that diet directly dictates consciousness through the practice of macrobiotics. He claims that eating meat fosters aggressive, war-like thoughts, while sugar and dairy promote laziness and sadness. Conversely, he recommends daily consumption of whole cereal grains (such as brown rice, wheat, and barley), arguing that because grains grow upward toward the sky, they naturally clear the mind and align human consciousness with the infinite universe.
