What is an elemental diet?

An elemental diet is a liquid, complete meal replacement formulated to be hypoallergenic and pre-digested. It is considered a medical food, not a typical protein shake.

It works on two key principles:

  • Remove food triggers that cause inflammation
  • Remove fuel that feeds imbalances like SIBO and fungal overgrowth

The diet provides rest for the gut and easy-to-absorb nutrients needed for healing.

Unlike many shakes, elemental diets avoid irritants such as emulsifiers, prebiotics, fibers, and sugar alcohols, which can damage the gut lining in sensitive individuals.

The pre-digested nature allows nutrients to be absorbed within the first two to three feet of the small intestine, starving pathogens like SIBO and fungus while allowing the remaining 20 feet of the gut to rest and heal.

How to Use the elemental diet

There are three protocols:

  1. Full reset: Use the elemental diet as your sole source of nutrition for one to three weeks.
  2. Hybrid (half and half): Replace roughly half of your daily calories with elemental shakes and consume normal whole foods for the other half.
  3. Short reset followed by hybrid: Begin with a short exclusive period (one to four days), then transition to the hybrid approach.

The hybrid approach has been studied for up to two years and can be beneficial for long-term gut health.

The greater the severity of symptoms, the more of the elemental diet one may use.

For example: - Severe, longstanding symptoms: Full two-week reset followed by hybrid. - Mild symptoms: Short reset (1–4 days) followed by hybrid.
