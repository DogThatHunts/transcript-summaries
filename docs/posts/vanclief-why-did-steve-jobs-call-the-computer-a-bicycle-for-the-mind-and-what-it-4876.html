Why Did Steve Jobs Call the Computer a Bicycle for the Mind?

VanClief quotes Steve Jobs as saying in 1990, during a documentary titled Memory and Imagination, that a computer is 'the equivalent of a bicycle for our minds.'

  • Jobs used the bicycle metaphor to emphasize that computers don't replace human decision-making, unlike tools focused solely on efficiency.
  • The bicycle, like a computer, empowers individuals to do more—not just work faster or replace human labor.
  • This view contrasts with the common interpretation that computers are merely efficient tools; instead, Jobs saw them as extensions that give people freedom to choose how to act.
