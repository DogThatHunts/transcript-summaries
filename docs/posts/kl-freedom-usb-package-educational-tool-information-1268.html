KL | Freedom USB Package Educational Tool Information

A guide to the contents, pricing structure, and strict distribution terms of the sovereign-themed Freedom USB educational package.

The host of Rice Against the Grain outlines the details of the Freedom USB package, a physical educational tool co-created with Dr. KL (also known as Mr. Beneficiary). Contents of the educational drive

The physical USB drive contains a collection of documents, books, and video lessons totaling nearly six hours of private instruction. The contents include: * Private lessons and documents: A "pregame" video and five core private lessons. * Public lessons: Earlier collaborative videos between the host and KL, including series on "Collateralizing America" and what the creators describe as "legal fiction trickery," alongside public lessons one through four. * Administrative guides: Video instructions and documents covering topics the creators identify as long- and short-form birth certificate authentication and Doing Business As (DBA) filings. * Reference library: Over 30 reference files, including editions of Black's Law Dictionary, Bouvier's Law Dictionary (1856), and The Art of Passing the Buck (Volume 1). * Community access: Entry into a private Telegram group reserved exclusively for current and former paid students of KL to collaborate and assist one another.

Distribution terms and anti-sharing measures

To maintain privacy and prevent unauthorized online distribution, the creators have established strict terms of service: * No online hosting: The files are distributed strictly peer-to-peer via physical USB drives. They are not hosted on cloud services or private servers. * Non-disclosure agreement: By purchasing the package, buyers agree not to upload the private videos or documents to the internet. * Verification system: To prevent users from sharing the USB drive to bypass payment, KL verifies all paperwork clients against the host's buyer log. If an individual attempts to submit paperwork to KL using a shared drive without having purchased the package directly, they will be required to pay the host the initial drive fee or pay KL a higher rate.

Pricing tiers and membership discounts

The pricing structure is divided into several categories based on marital status and existing memberships: * Standard single rate: $500 for the USB drive, followed by an $800 fee paid to KL for paperwork completion, totaling $1,300. * Married couples: $500 for the USB drive, with a minimum paperwork fee of $1,600 paid to KL ($800 per person), totaling $2,100. The host specifies this rate applies to traditional male-female marriages. * RiceTVX lifetime members: Active lifetime members of RiceTVX.com (Rice Uncensored) receive a $50 discount, reducing the USB price to $450. * Non-member bundle: New buyers can purchase lifetime access to Rice Uncensored alongside the USB drive for $550. * Current/former KL students: Existing paid students of KL can purchase the USB drive for $250, which includes complimentary lifetime access to Rice Uncensored. * International shipping: An additional $20 shipping fee is required for orders outside the United States.

Accepted payment methods and ordering instructions

The host does not accept digital fiat payment platforms such as PayPal, Cash App, or standard personal and bank checks. Instead, accepted payment methods are limited to: * United States Postal Service (USPS) money orders (convenience store or bank money orders are explicitly rejected). * Physical cash (requires direct coordination). * Gold and silver. * Bitcoin and select other cryptocurrencies.

To order, prospective students must email ricemediagroup@protonmail.com with "USB" in the subject line.
