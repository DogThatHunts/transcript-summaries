A guided manifesting technique using subconscious reprogramming through visualization, relaxation, and a six-repetition affirmation practice.

Repeat this phrase six times: your subconscious will do the rest

Overview

SecretWithin presents a structured manifesting method rooted in what he attributes to José Silva's research on altered mental states. The core argument is that most people remain trapped in "beta"—a state of surface-level, scattered thinking—and never access the deeper "Z-state" where the subconscious is fully receptive to reprogramming. SecretWithin claims that by entering this Z-state and repeating a specific intention six times with feeling and visualization, listeners can align their external reality with their deepest desires.

The foundational premise

SecretWithin argues that visualization is the beginning of creation: everything must first exist in the mind before it can materialize in the physical world. He contends that 99% of people never unlock their desires because they remain trapped in a mental state that prevents access to "hidden power" already within them. The proposed solution is neither positive thinking nor surface-level affirmations, but rather a process of reconfiguring the mind to align with one's dreams—a process he claims takes less than one minute.

Mental states: beta, alpha, and z

SecretWithin distinguishes three levels of consciousness:

  • Beta: The chaotic, accelerated surface level of everyday thinking, characterized by stress, constant distraction, and jumping thoughts. He compares this to planting seeds in dry, barren soil—no lasting transformation occurs here.
  • Alpha: A bridge between conscious and subconscious mind, described as a hidden state that can bypass limitations and dissolve fear.
  • Z-state: The deepest level, where the subconscious fully opens "like fertile soil ready to be planted." In this state, barriers of the conscious mind dissolve, and beliefs, emotions, and desires can be programmed with "astonishing effectiveness." Thoughts become "clear instructions to reality" rather than fleeting ideas.

SecretWithin attributes these concepts to discoveries made in the 1940s, though he does not provide specific citations or evidence.

The four-step process

Step 1: relax the body to calm the mind

Find a quiet, undisturbed space. Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and take deep breaths—holding briefly, then exhaling with intention. Repeat until the weight of the day fades. Focus on your eyelids growing heavy, then allow relaxation to flow through your face, neck, shoulders, arms, chest, and legs until your entire body feels light. This is described as "the doorway to Z."

Step 2: the visualization countdown

Imagine standing before a staircase with 10 steps. Descend while counting backward from 10 to 1, feeling yourself sink deeper into calm with each step. At the final step, you see a door representing the entrance to the Z-state. Place your hand on it, feel its energy, and when ready, open it. As you step through, you enter "a space of infinite stillness" where time fades and noise disappears. This is where reprogramming begins.

Step 3: Repeat the intention six times

Now in this receptive state, anchor your intention by visualizing what you desire most, seeing it approaching you, and feeling gratitude, relief, and joy. The phrase to repeat six times is:

"Everything I desire is already finding its way to me. I am open to receive with gratitude and trust."

SecretWithin emphasizes that this is not about saying words but about feeling them. Each repetition is described as a transformation; by the sixth repetition, you are no longer asking but knowing.

Step 4: seal it with gratitude

Before opening your eyes, pause and take a deep breath. Say quietly: "Thank you for the abundance that is already mine." SecretWithin argues that gratitude is "the bridge between intention and reality," shifting you from waiting to receiving.

Why six repetitions?

SecretWithin claims the number six is not arbitrary but based on principles that amplify mental programming. The subconscious learns through repetition, but there is a "sweet spot"—enough repetition to imprint the message without creating resistance. Six repetitions, he argues, reach that point perfectly.

The power of silence

SecretWithin emphasizes that after performing this process, one must maintain silence about the intention. He argues that explaining your intention to others drags it back into the conscious, analytical mind—"the very level where doubt, judgment, and resistance live." Silence, he contends, is not secrecy but protection. When an intention remains internal, it continues working below awareness, uninterrupted and unchallenged. This is why results often appear suddenly and effortlessly, seeming like coincidence to others.

Trust over force

SecretWithin stresses that the key is repetition and absolute trust. While the subconscious reorganizes perception, attention, and behavior to match the planted instruction, you should not feel it working—awareness would only slow it down. He emphasizes trust over force: do not chase outcomes, do not speak about them, and allow them to unfold. When results appear, they will seem like coincidence, but the practitioner will know otherwise.

Closing claim

SecretWithin does not ask for belief but invites listeners to test the method themselves. He claims that if applied exactly as explained and allowed to work without interference, "the results will speak louder than any words ever could."
