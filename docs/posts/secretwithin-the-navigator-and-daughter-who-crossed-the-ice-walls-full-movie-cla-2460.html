Opening: William morris's journey begins

In the late 1700s, a former Continental Navy navigator named William Morris hears cryptic stories from his captain, Butler, about a passage through the Antarctic ice walls—a gateway to lands beyond. Years later, driven by obsession and a mysterious warning from a man named Fint about shadowy "Custodians," William buys a ship and assembles a crew. Despite threats and Butler's disappearance, Butler reappears with maps and supplies, urging immediate departure. The crew sets sail from Charleston in November, heading south into the unknown.

The crossing and first contact

After months at sea, William's ship reaches a massive ice wall, 90 meters high, glowing red and gold. Butler navigates them through a narrow passage, and they emerge into calm waters beyond. A colossal craft approaches, crewed by beings in white suits bearing strange blue emblems. They greet the ship and hand Butler a map, warning of "unsafe lands" and directing them toward the "Republic." The encounter leaves the crew unsettled, aware they have entered dangerous territory.

The Ancestral Republic and revelation

The crew arrives at a glowing city called El Arca, part of what is called the Ancestral Republic. President Phale meets William and reveals a shocking truth: humanity has been "reset" many times. When those in power fear losing control, they destroy most of the population and restart civilization with the youngest survivors. William's people are "old humanity"—from an older cycle. The President warns that their crossing has drawn dangerous attention.

That night, the city comes under siege. Strange silver spheres hover overhead; blue triangles streak across the sky. William helps evacuate civilians and boards a ship with Butler, escaping the burning city. They travel through tunnels and emerge above rolling fields and orchards—a thriving land untouched by the pollution of the known world. Butler reveals that the Custodians deliberately poison the air in William's homeland to shorten lifespans and prevent questioning.

Butler's island and the truth unfolds

The ship reaches Butler's island, where he reunites with his family. That evening, Butler tells William the full truth: the Custodians have conditioned humanity's minds through constant noise, wars, and illusions of choice. They have hidden humanity's true history and kept people chained without visible restraints. William realizes the weight of this knowledge—if people knew the truth, they would rise up.

The lands of the Anakim and ancient history

The crew travels to the lands of the Anakim (giants), where they meet Sheike, a tall woman with golden hair. She shows William an enormous book revealing 178 circle environments (worlds) within a Great Dome, plus countless undiscovered lands. She explains that the Custodians and Anunnaki are colonizers who move from world to world, consuming them under an ancient treaty. The Celestial Lands, however, remain untouched—the Custodians' power fails there, destroyed by a conscious force called the Source.

Sheike describes how the Custodians tried to capture the Source through blood rituals and wars, but failed. They then used resets to weaken humanity, erasing memory and potential with each cycle. She reveals the history of Great Tartary, an alliance of humans and giants that nearly defeated the Custodians, but was ultimately crushed. The giants retreated, fearing annihilation if they defied the Custodians again.

William's final years and the infiltration plan

William settles in the Republic, eventually becoming commander-in-chief of their naval forces. He witnesses a harmonious society free from material greed, crime, and hierarchical rule. He learns that the Republic's strength lies in "death of the ego"—freedom from identity traps that enslave the mind.

Butler and President Phale devise a plan to awaken those trapped in the known lands. Direct confrontation is too risky. Instead, they will use the "Summer Portal," an overlooked entry point, to slip ancestral teachers into the known lands undetected. These teachers will spread subtle whispers of freedom, hints of other lands, and spiritual techniques to break conditioning. Over years, 14,000 infiltrators work in secret, weaving influence into politics, institutions, and key areas.

However, near the Antarctic Peninsula, an encounter with a Custodian military base forces infiltrators to sink their ship in escape. The Custodians launch a manhunt and raids. Many infiltrators are captured and tortured. William, aboard a ship called Iron Blue during a return mission, is killed when the Custodians create an invisible barrier near the dome, obliterating the vessel mid-air.

Helen Morris and the continuation

William's daughter, Helen, is born in the Republic to a woman native to those lands. She grows up hearing stories of her father's courage and her mother's ancestral sacrifices. Returning to the known lands as an adult, Helen finds them unchanged, still trapped under Custodian control. Wars continue, not for freedom but to perpetuate division and control. She carries her father's message: true security cannot come from governments or leaders—it is up to each person to break free from those who pull strings from the shadows.

The Great Dome and the cycle of control

The narrative describes the Great Dome as a colossal structure enclosing 178 circle environments. Travel between worlds is difficult; portals are unstable and dangerous. Independent lands exist between environments, once used as resting points by early explorers. The Dome holds secrets and challenges, and crossing it comes at a price few are willing to pay.

The Great war and the Last Reset

Long ago, humans and giants united against the Custodians in what became known as Great Tartary—a thriving civilization of innovation and unity. The Custodians saw this as an existential threat and launched a devastating war. Despite fierce resistance and moments when victory seemed possible, the alliance was ultimately crushed. The Custodians reshaped the known lands, creating ice walls and barriers, and erasing all memory of Tartary.

The Last Reset was catastrophic. Adults and children over seven months old were wiped out; only infants, carefully chosen by the Custodians, were spared. These survivors grew in a world engineered to stifle spiritual and intellectual growth. The Custodians controlled every aspect of life, ensuring humanity never again understood its true potential or broke free.

Humanity's unique connection to the Source—an essence no other race possesses—makes it a pivotal force in the struggle for freedom.

The journey is long, the struggle immense, but the dream of liberated humanity burns brighter than ever. Those who fight do so knowing that seeds of unity and love will one day bloom, ushering in a world free from tyranny.
