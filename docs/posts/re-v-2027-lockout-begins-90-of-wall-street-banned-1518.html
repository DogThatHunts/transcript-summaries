The 2027 Wall Street housing ban

RE-V discusses the "21st Century Road to Housing Act," a bipartisan bill passed by Congress that bans institutional investors owning 350 or more homes from purchasing additional single-family properties. This federal ban is set to take effect in January 2027 and will run for 15 years until 2042. To enforce the law, companies must report their direct and indirect holdings annually to the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD). Violators face severe penalties, including fines of $1 million per home or three times the home's purchase price.

The pivot to build-to-rent communities

To bypass the restrictions of the new legislation, RE-V explains that major Wall Street landlords are shifting their strategies toward "build-to-rent" (BTR) developments. The 21st Century Road to Housing Act contains loopholes allowing institutional investors to acquire newly constructed rental properties, build communities from the ground up, or purchase substantially rehabilitated homes. For example, American Homes for Rent (AMH) has transitioned into a top 40 U.S. home builder, while Invitation Homes acquired the builder ResiBuilt in 2026.
