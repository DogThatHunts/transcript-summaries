Dave Champion | 7 Reasons To NEVER Get An mRNA Injection!

Physiologist Dave Champion outlines his biological and legal objections to mRNA injections while critiquing mainstream media censorship and challenging the legitimacy of the federal income tax.

Dave Champion argues that the public has been systematically denied the right to informed consent regarding mRNA products. He asserts that governments, pharmaceutical companies, and mainstream media outlets have coordinated to suppress independent scientific research detailing the potential harms of mRNA technology.

The six biological concerns of mrna technology

Champion outlines six primary product-based reasons to reject mRNA injections, arguing that the biological mechanisms of these products pose inherent risks to human health:

  • The dosing problem: Champion explains that unlike traditional therapeutics with controlled dosages, mRNA injections rely on the recipient's body to produce the antigen. He argues this creates an unpredictable and uncontrollable immune response, which can vary drastically between individuals.
  • Lipid nanoparticle biodistribution: He disputes early claims that lipid nanoparticles remain at the injection site, asserting that they travel throughout the entire body, including sensitive areas like the brain and reproductive organs.
  • Autoimmune destruction of healthy cells: Champion claims that when healthy cells absorb mRNA and produce spike proteins, the body's immune system sends T-cells to attack and destroy those otherwise healthy cells, leading to organ inflammation and damage.
  • Plasmid DNA contamination: He highlights findings from independent and FDA-affiliated laboratories showing that mRNA products contain levels of plasmid DNA that exceed federal regulatory limits, which he argues could interfere with the host's genetic management.
  • Increased susceptibility to infection: Champion cites statistical analyses that he claims show vaccinated individuals are more likely to contract SARS-CoV-2 than those who did not receive the injection.
  • Lack of memory T-cell production: He asserts that mRNA injections fail to stimulate the production of memory T-cells, which he argues is the reason health authorities recommend frequent booster shots.

The legal and institutional barriers to safety

Beyond biological concerns, Champion points to institutional and legal structures that he believes compromise vaccine safety and public awareness:

  • Manufacturer liability shields: Champion maintains a personal rule never to accept a medical product if Congress has shielded its manufacturer from legal liability. He argues that immunity from lawsuits disincentivizes corporate executives from investigating or acting upon safety warning signs during trials.
  • Financial influence over media and journals: He claims that mainstream media outlets are financially dependent on pharmaceutical advertising—which he estimates accounts for up to 75% of television ad revenue—preventing them from reporting on vaccine risks. He also asserts that medical journals have actively suppressed or retracted valid scientific papers that contradict official government narratives.

Metabolic health and viral resistance

Champion argues that the perceived severity of the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic was amplified by the poor baseline metabolic health of the American public. Drawing on his background in physiology, he advocates for metabolic fitness as the primary defense against viral illness. He attributes his own robust health and resistance to disease to maintaining a state of ketosis and performing regular physical training, rather than relying on pharmaceutical interventions.

Challenging the federal income tax system

In the final portion of his presentation, Champion transitions to a critique of the U.S. tax system, promoting his book Income Tax: Shattering the Myths. He claims that after three decades of studying tax statutes and regulations, he has concluded that Congress has never legally imposed an income tax on ordinary working Americans. Champion openly states that he has not filed a tax return or paid federal income tax in 32 years, asserting that his understanding of the law protects him from federal prosecution.
