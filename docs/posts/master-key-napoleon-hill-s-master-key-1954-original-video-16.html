Napoleon Hill introduces his "Master Key" philosophy, promising that if one defines their greatest desire and writes it down, they will receive the key to its attainment. This is the first of 13 messages, each revealing aspects of this key. The foundational natural law, discovered by psychologists, is: "Whatever the mind can conceive and believe, the mind can achieve." Hill emphasizes that this requires no formal education, citing Thomas A. Edison as an example.

Andrew Carnegie's influence and the two sealed envelopes

Hill recounts how Andrew Carnegie inspired his search for the power behind this law, leading to the discovery of the Master Key. Carnegie's lecture, which changed Hill's life, highlighted the profound power of controlling one's own mind and directing it. Carnegie stated that everyone is born with the privilege of controlling their mind power, and with it, the equivalent of two "sealed envelopes."

Riches (if you take possession of your mind): Sound health, peace of mind, a labor of love of your own choice, freedom from fear and worry, a positive mental attitude, and material riches of your own choice and quantity.

Penalties (if you neglect your mind): Ill health, fear and worry, indecision and doubt, frustration and discouragement, poverty and want, and a host of evils including envy, greed, jealousy, anger, hatred, and superstition.

Instructions for attaining riches

Hill provides simple instructions: 1. Procure a pocket-sized notebook. On page one, write a clear description of your major desire in life. 2. On page two, write a clear statement of what you intend to give in return for that desire, and start giving immediately. 3. Memorize both statements and repeat them at least a dozen times daily, ending with a prayer of gratitude for the gift of mind control.

Principle 2: the master mind principle

This principle involves two or more people working in perfect harmony for a definite purpose. Hill explains its importance: * It allows one to borrow and use the education, experience, influence, and capital of others. * It enables accomplishments in one year that would take a lifetime alone. * Hill suggests the first known application was between Jesus and his 12 disciples. * A true mastermind alliance, in perfect harmony, draws upon spiritual forces and offers protection against failure, provided the purpose is beneficial to all. * Hill's research showed that successful individuals and even the American way of life (e.g., Declaration of Independence, US Federation of States) are built on this principle. * Examples: Kate Smith's singing career flourished after forming an alliance with Ted Collins; Edgar Bergen and Charlie McCarthy gained fame through a mastermind alliance; Henry Ford's industrial empire began with an alliance with his wife, who provided faith and courage. * Application: For those working for a salary, forming a mastermind alliance with co-workers and management can lead to higher income and positions. Hill promises to show how to "write your own price tag." * Immediate steps: Decide your three-year goals, desired income and what you'll do to earn it, and form a mastermind alliance with family and colleagues. * Crucial reminder: Control your mental attitude to be friendly and agreeable for cooperation. Indifference or a negative attitude leads to failure. Success is planned, earned, and often achieved by taking others along.

This principle is about "rendering more service and better service than one is expected to render and doing it in a positive mental attitude." Hill calls it the "QQMA formula": Quality + Quantity + Mental Attitude determines one's position and compensation. * Benefits (15 points): Attracts favorable attention, invokes the law of increasing returns, makes one indispensable, fosters excellence, provides job security, benefits from the law of contrast, improves personality, develops imagination, inspires personal initiative, builds self-reliance, overcomes procrastination, earns respect and cooperation, develops definiteness of purpose, justifies requests for promotion/higher pay, and conditions the mind for mastermind alliances. * Hill states he has never received a major favorable break without applying this principle. If a position doesn't allow for going the extra mile, he advises changing positions. * Nature's example: A farmer goes the extra mile by preparing the ground and planting before compensation, and nature returns a hundredfold. * Test: For seven days, render unasked-for service to someone nearby, with a pleasing mental attitude, without expectation of compensation. Observe the changed atmosphere and increased happiness.
