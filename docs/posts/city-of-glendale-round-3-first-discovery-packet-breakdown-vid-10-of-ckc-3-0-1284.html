Dual-instrument theory of municipal utility bills

Williams outlines his primary factual theory regarding municipal utility bills sent to customers:

  • Top main section (bearer draft): The top portion of a municipal bill contains an order to pay and a due date but names no payee, defaulting to a bearer draft under UCC § 3-205(b) negotiable by possession alone.
  • Bottom detachable stub (order draft or coupon): The remittance stub states a specific payee ("City of Glendale") and contains processing instructions, functioning as a separate order draft or discount voucher.
  • Routing investigation: The discovery strategy aims to trace how municipal entities handle, separate, and route these two sections independently after receiving them from customers.
