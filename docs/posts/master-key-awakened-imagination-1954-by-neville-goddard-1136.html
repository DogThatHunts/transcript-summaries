Master Key | Awakened Imagination (1954) by Neville Goddard

A structured exploration of Neville Goddard's classic text on the transformative power of human imagination, inner speech, and the art of mental revision to shape physical reality.

Chapter 1: who is your imagination?

Neville Goddard redefines imagination, arguing it is not a flight of fancy but the eternal, spiritual core of humanity, which he equates with Jesus Christ. He asserts that the physical universe is merely a shadow of this inner power. According to Goddard, true living begins when one recognizes that imagination is the gateway to reality, enabling individuals to overcome limitations and achieve their goals by identifying with their ideals.

Chapter 2: world instructions

Goddard describes his transition from traditional religious views to a psychological understanding of scripture, a shift catalyzed by his mentor, Abdullah. He shares a personal experience from 1933 where he successfully traveled to Barbados by mentally living "from" the destination rather than merely thinking "of" it. He argues that the external world is a projection of internal states of consciousness, and that physical circumstances naturally align with one's habitual beliefs.

Chapter 3: highways of the inner world

This chapter contrasts the natural, sensory man with the spiritual, imaginative man. Goddard explains that the inner body operates in a higher space and that its movements are causal, while outer physical movements are merely reactionary. He details a practical exercise: immobilizing the physical body and mentally enacting an action that implies the fulfillment of a desire. He shares the story of a blind woman who used this technique to secure daily car rides to work, demonstrating how inner actions produce outer results.

Chapter 4: the pruning shears of revision

Goddard introduces the concept of "revision" as a daily practice of mentally rewriting unfavorable events to match one's ideals. He frames this as the true, practical application of forgiveness. By mentally altering disappointing news or interactions before sleep, individuals can change their future and even experience physical healing, as illustrated by an artist who healed her injured foot by revising her day.

Chapter 5: the coin of heaven

The focus turns to "inner speech"—the silent mental conversations that reflect an individual's state of consciousness. Goddard argues that these thoughts are the "coin of heaven" and must be invested wisely rather than spent on negative emotions. He asserts that future events are shaped by this internal dialogue, and that changing one's life requires consciously adopting the inner speech of a fulfilled desire.

Chapter 7: creation is finished

Goddard presents the metaphysical claim that all possible human situations and states of consciousness already exist as completed, eternal possibilities. He recounts a mystical experience in which he temporarily froze time and witnessed the external world stop. He concludes that human attention is what animates these pre-existing states, and that individuals can navigate to new realities by changing their beliefs and matching their mental activity to their desired state.

Chapter 8: the apple of god's eye

In the final chapter, Goddard summarizes his teachings, defining the birth of Christ as the awakening of the second, imaginative man. He urges readers to practice the art of revision and conscious inner speech, promising that those who align their inner actions with their ideals will experience a transformed life and realize their divine nature.
