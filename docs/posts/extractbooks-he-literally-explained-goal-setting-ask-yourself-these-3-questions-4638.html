Three Questions to clarify your goals

ExtractBooks shares a powerful goal-setting method rooted in reflection and values.

  • What would you do if you won a million dollars and had unlimited time? This reveals your deepest desires.
  • What would you stop doing or do more of if you only had six months to live? This exposes your core values.
  • What one great thing would you dare to dream if you knew you could not fail? This identifies your life’s purpose.

Writing down answers helps solidify goals and aligns actions with personal values.
