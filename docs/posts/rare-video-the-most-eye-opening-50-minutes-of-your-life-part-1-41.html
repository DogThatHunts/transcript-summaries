Overview of occult knowledge and the arcana

Speaker Mark Passio defines the "occult" as hidden knowledge regarding the human psyche and the laws of nature—both physical laws (such as gravity and electromagnetism) and spiritual or moral laws governing behavioral consequences. He asserts that dark occultists deliberately withhold this knowledge to create a power differential between themselves and the general public.

Passio divides occult knowledge into two primary categories: - Minor Arcana (minor knowledge): Focuses on the individual scale, examining the human psyche, subconscious motivations, emotions, and internal mechanisms that drive personal actions. - Major Arcana (major knowledge): Examines macrocosmic forces, natural laws, and universal moral principles that govern the outcomes of actions for conscious beings.

According to Passio, suppressing these principles leaves individuals vulnerable to manipulation and unable to govern themselves effectively.

Mental and worldview schisms

Passio argues that dark occultism maintains societal control by keeping humanity in states of chronic psychological and philosophical imbalance:

The mental schism

  • Left-brain imbalance (masculine/logic-dominated): Leads to hyper-skepticism, scientism, atheism, rejection of objective truth, social Darwinism, moral relativism, and support for authoritarian control or eugenics ("master think").
  • Right-brain imbalance (feminine/creativity-dominated): Leads to naivete, blind faith, religious extremism, solipsism, order-following, and feelings of unworthiness ("slave think").

Passio states that power structures play these two imbalanced states off against each other to prevent holistic, balanced consciousness.

The worldview schism

  • Randomness worldview: Views the universe as a purpose-free accident with no spiritual or moral laws, reducing life to mere survivalism.
  • Deterministic worldview: Asserts that everything is predetermined and human free will is an illusion, leading to passivity, fatalism, and spiritual inaction.
  • The synthesis: Passio posits that reality functions through a combination of fixed, unchangeable natural laws (deterministic consequences) and conscious choice (free will).

Collective co-creation, the trivium, and spiritual currencies

Passio critiques the popular New Age interpretation of the "law of attraction," asserting that individuals do not create their reality in isolation. Instead, reality is co-created collectively through shared choices, knowledge processing, and actions.

To describe how truth is systematically processed, Passio presents the ancient trivium method: 1. Grammar (input / knowledge): Gathering raw, available information (the opposite being total ignorance). 2. Logic (processing / understanding): Filtering and making sense of data within the human mind (the opposite being confusion). 3. Rhetoric (output / wisdom): Translating understanding into right action in the physical world (the opposite being folly).

Passio identifies time and attention as the ultimate "spiritual currencies." Investing time and attention into pure, truth-resonating information generates true value and spiritual vision. Conversely, consuming distraction, entertainment, or distorted information leads to collective degradation. He further asserts that fiat currency and precious metals lack intrinsic value, framing money primarily as a mechanism that acts as resistance against systemic change.

The five expressions of natural law

Passio outlines a five-tier framework detailing how internal choices manifest into external societal conditions:

  1. Generative polarity (love vs. fear): - Love acts as an expansive force that opens consciousness to truth. - Fear acts as a contractive force that shuts down awareness and fosters unconsciousness.

  2. Initiating expression (knowledge vs. ignorance): - Choosing love leads to knowledge through accepting truth. - Choosing fear results in ignorance (the deliberate refusal of available truth, distinct from nescience, which is a passive lack of information).

  3. Internal expression (sovereignty vs. confusion): - Processing knowledge leads to sovereignty (internal monarchy), where an individual's thoughts, emotions, and actions align without internal contradiction. - Ignorance produces confusion (internal anarchy), marked by internal division and a lack of self-mastery.

  4. External expression (freedom vs. control): - Collective sovereignty manifests as freedom (external anarchy), defined as the absence of masters and slaves. - Collective confusion leads to external control and totalitarianism.

  5. Manifestation (order vs. chaos): - Adherence to natural law and truth produces order (manifested goodness, justice, and cooperative spiritual anarchy). - Direct opposition to natural law produces chaos (manifested evil and psychopathic social conditions).

Passio concludes that order cannot be achieved without aligning human behavior with the objective principles of natural law.
