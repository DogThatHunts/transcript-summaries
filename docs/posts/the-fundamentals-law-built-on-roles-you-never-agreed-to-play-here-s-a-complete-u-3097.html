Layer 2: the statutory overlay and artificial constructs

The second layer is the statutory overlay, consisting of rules, taxes, licenses, and registrations enacted by parliament. The author argues that this resulting trust is not a single event occurring at birth registration. Instead, it is a recurrent resulting trust that triggers every time value, labor, or property is moved toward a statutory construct. Consequently, while the construct may hold the legal title, the beneficial interest continuously results back to the living individual, leaving the construct as an empty shell.
