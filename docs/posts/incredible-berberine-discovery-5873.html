Incredible Berberine Discovery

Berberin and metformin are two molecules used to lower blood sugar. While metformin has long been the focus of pharmaceutical research, recent studies have uncovered a new mechanism of action involving the intestines.

  • The traditional understanding was that these molecules reduce glucose production in the liver.
  • New research shows that berberin and metformin interact with intestinal epithelial cells, altering their metabolism.
  • These cells typically use fat metabolism to generate energy in mitochondria, but berberin and metformin inhibit this process.
  • As a result, the cells switch to glucose metabolism, consuming dietary carbohydrates before they enter the bloodstream.
  • This creates a metabolic sink that helps prevent blood sugar spikes, particularly beneficial for people with insulin resistance.
