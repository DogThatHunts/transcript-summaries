Why your Plan b Could Be Making you Fail

ExtractBooks discusses how having a fallback plan—like going back to school or returning to live with parents—can undermine your full commitment to a venture.

  • A 'plan B' creates psychological distance from your primary goal, reducing motivation and effort.
  • Research shows people with a plan B perform worse on tasks when given a reward, as they feel less committed.
  • The mindset of 'if I fail, I'll go back to Stanford' or 'I'll live with my parents' signals half-hearted engagement from the start.
  • Full commitment comes from believing there is no escape, not from having a backup option.
