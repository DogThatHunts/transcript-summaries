Will Barron | How to Grow Your Business So Fast People Assume You Got Lucky

Four simple sales systems that turn service businesses into high-growth machines, making founders look like they got lucky

The two categories of service-based businesses

Every service-based business can be divided into two groups: those that grow slowly through hard work, and those that grow rapidly—so fast that people assume they got lucky.

The four sales systems for Fast growth

Will Barron identifies four foundational sales systems that enable service-based businesses to grow quickly and predictably.

Lead gen system

  • Most founders fail because they try too many lead generation channels at once.
  • A single, focused channel allows for clear measurement of results.
  • Will shares that when he ran ads, YouTube videos, webinars, and cold emails simultaneously, his revenue dropped to $20,000/month.
  • He fixed this by cutting back to just YouTube and achieved a return to 100k/month revenue with 90% profit.
  • His recommended approach:
  • Set a specific lead goal with clear qualification criteria.
  • Pick one lead gen channel and test it long enough to measure results.
  • Either keep, fix, or drop the channel before moving to the next.
  • Each channel takes time to improve—don’t expect success on the first try.

Offer system

  • Most service business offers are 'average' because they promise generic deliverables (e.g., PDFs, one-to-one calls).
  • An effective offer must deliver a clear outcome and remove risk for the buyer.
  • Will compares two offers:
  • A typical offer: 10 one-to-one calls, a PDF, and a portal with training.
  • His offer at Salesman.com: Guarantee to increase a client’s sales team close rate in 30 days—or money back.
  • The key difference is outcome-based, not deliverable-based.
  • To evaluate your offer, ask four questions:
  • Is it safe for the buyer to buy?
  • Does the buyer see results fast?
  • Is it easy to work with?
  • Is the return on investment obvious?
  • Will’s offer answers all four: it includes a guarantee, fast delivery of a bespoke sales operating system, minimal effort for the client, and a clear financial ROI.

Sales handoff system

  • Founders who personally close every deal have built a job, not a business.
  • When hiring salespeople without a system, results are poor and blame is placed on the rep.
  • Will shares a client case: Asher hired three reps, each closed only one deal while Asher closed five.
  • After a call with Will, Asher implemented a full sales operating system with daily activities and clear performance targets.
  • The rep remains with the business two years later, and revenue continues to grow.
  • Before hiring a salesperson, ask:
  • Is there a step-by-step daily plan?
  • What revenue number indicates a profitable asset?
  • How long do you give them to hit that number before letting them go?

Retention system

  • Most founders follow up ad hoc, leaving money on the table.
  • A proven retention system:
  • Send an end-of-engagement report showing the ROI delivered.
  • Book a meeting to discuss new problems that have emerged.
  • Offer to solve the new problem (e.g., increasing lifetime value, moving up market).
  • Will shares that clients are excited to explore further because they see tangible results.
  • Retention must be documented and followed for every client.
  • Without it, businesses lose tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars in potential revenue.
