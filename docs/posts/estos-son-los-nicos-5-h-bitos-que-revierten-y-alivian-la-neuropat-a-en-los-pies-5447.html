Control glucémico de precisión

Dr. Rivera emphasizes the importance of controlling glucose variability, not just average glucose levels. He recommends:

  • Eating protein and fiber before carbohydrates to reduce post-meal glucose spikes.
  • Walking 10-15 minutes after meals to improve glucose absorption.
  • Drinking apple cider vinegar before meals to slow carbohydrate absorption.

Restauración de la microcirculación

Improving blood flow to the nerves is critical for neuropathy recovery. Dr. Rivera suggests:

  • Exercise: Moderate aerobic exercise for 30 minutes, 5 times a week, to produce nitric oxide.
  • Temperature Contrast: Alternating between warm and cold water baths to improve microcirculation.
  • Foot Position: Keeping feet at heart level during rest to optimize blood flow.

Remielinización con nutrición de precisión

Nutrient deficiencies can worsen neuropathy. Dr. Rivera recommends:

  • Vitamin B12 (methylcobalamin): 1500-3000 micrograms daily.
  • Alpha-lipoic acid: 600 mg daily, taken on an empty stomach.
  • Vitamin D3 with K2: 2000-5000 IU daily with 100-200 micrograms of K2.

Regulación del sistema nervioso autónomo

Managing stress and improving sleep quality can significantly impact neuropathy. Dr. Rivera advises:

  • Diaphragmatic Breathing: 4-4-6 or 4-4-8 breathing pattern to reduce cortisol levels.
  • Quality Sleep: Ensuring complete darkness, optimal room temperature, and consistent sleep schedule.

Neuroplasticidad periférica diada

Sensory stimulation can improve neuropathy by maintaining active nerve pathways. Dr. Rivera recommends:

  • Progressive Sensory Stimulation: Using different textures and temperatures on the feet.
  • Proprioceptive Exercises: Balancing on one foot to improve proprioception and reduce fall risk.
  • Neuropathic Massage: Specific foot massage to improve lymphatic and venous return.

Implementation plan

Dr. Rivera suggests a progressive 4-week plan to implement the habits:

  • Week 1: Control glucémico and precise nutrition.
  • Week 2: Add microcirculation improvement.
  • Week 3: Start stress regulation and sleep improvement.
  • Week 4: Incorporate sensory stimulation and proprioceptive exercises.

Conclusion

By following these five habits, individuals can create the right environment for nerve regeneration and potentially reverse neuropathy. Dr. Rivera encourages viewers to track their progress over 90 days.
