5 Rules I Live by

Here are five landlord lessons I learned managing over 100 rental units.

  • Rent is due at 5 p.m. on the first, with no exceptions. Charge a late fee every time to prevent tenants from learning to delay payments.
  • Let every tenant call go to voicemail. Listen to the message first—only then decide if it’s a real emergency. A missing light bulb can wait; a burst pipe cannot.
  • You can’t do it alone. Assign a helper to handle routine tasks like knocking on doors and posting notices, so you can focus on major decisions.
  • Price rent about 10% below market to keep units full, while collecting the maximum legal deposit. A large deposit encourages tenants to take better care of the property.
  • Screen every applicant thoroughly, as the moment they get the keys, your leverage shifts. Always check eviction records and contact their previous landlord.
