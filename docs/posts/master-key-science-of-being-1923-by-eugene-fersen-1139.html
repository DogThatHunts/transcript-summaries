Master Key | Science of Being (1923) by Eugene Fersen

Eugene Fersen presents a metaphysical system that synthesizes cosmic laws, magnetic energy exercises, mental power, and spiritual evolution to guide human self-realization.

Overview

Eugene Fersen’s Science of Being (1923) outlines a metaphysical philosophy that bridges esoteric science, physical wellness, and spiritual evolution. Fersen redefines divinity as "The Great Principle"—an eternal, all-pervading first cause—and presents practical exercises, mental disciplines, and cosmological laws designed to harmonize human consciousness with universal energy.

Section beats

Lesson 1: the Absolute and the Great Principle

  • Redefines divinity scientifically as the "Great Principle"—that which creates, constitutes, governs, sustains, and contains all existence.
  • Outlines the four primary aspects of the Great Principle: life, mind, truth, and love, which converge geometrically to form the apex of spirit.
  • Defines the three attributes of the Absolute as omnipresence, omniscience, and omnipotence, while contrasting the Egyptian, Hindu, and Christian concepts of the Trinity.
  • Frames the relationship between Christ (expressing divine love) and Lucifer (expressing fallen intellect seeking redemption).

Lesson 2: universal life energy and the Star Exercise

  • Explains primal energy as an all-pervading force operating through motion, attraction, and continuous vibration.
  • Distinguishes between physical (magnetic), mental, and spiritual vibration rates.
  • Introduces the physical method for drawing universal energy: the Star Exercise, a pentagram body posture with the left palm facing up to receive force and the right palm facing down to ground it.
  • Details energy's self-governing and harmonious properties, illustrating its use in emotional balancing, self-regulation, and magnetic healing.

Lesson 3: the relative nature of matter and the soul

  • Demonstrates through physics that physical matter is condensed primal energy built from molecules, atoms, and radioactive electrons.
  • Defines matter fundamentally as a state of consciousness—the sensory perception of energy effects.
  • Distinguishes between the transient human personality and the immortal, individualized soul.
  • Explains the mechanics of incarnation, bodily gestation, physical death, and reincarnation as progressive steps in soul evolution.

Lesson 4: practical application of life energy, auras, and rejuvenation

  • Details the three layers of the human aura: the etheric double, the inner aura, and the colored outer aura.
  • Outlines practical applications of magnetic current in daily life, professional success, and therapeutic healing (focusing energy through the seventh cervical vertebra).
  • Explains the induction of therapeutic magnetic sleep as distinct from hypnotic suggestion.
  • Provides a daily mental and energetic exercise for cellular rejuvenation to counteract aging and physical breakdown.

Lesson 5: Absolute and relative laws

  • Differentiates between three eternal laws of the Absolute (Spirit is all; the same law governs macrocosm and microcosm; all is vibration) and four mutable laws of the relative (polarity, rhythm, gender, and cause and effect).
  • Demonstrates how to neutralize the law of polarity (duality) by anchoring intention in the Absolute, and how to navigate the law of rhythm (oscillations) through conscious timing.
  • Explores the law of cause and effect (karma) and stresses the necessity of operating in the "ever-present now."
  • Introduces the law of love (the active neutralization of negative force) and the law of evolution (the intrinsic upward drive of all vibration).

Lesson 6: mind force, thought concentration, and telepathy

  • Analyzes mind force as self-conscious energy operated through the brain and the pituitary body (the telepathic apparatus).
  • Differentiates between ordinary diffuse thought waves ("thought dust") and focused, one-pointed creative thoughts.
  • Traces the three stages of a concentrated thought: direct telepathic impact, cultivation within the recipient's subconsciousness, and the eventual "boomerang" return of the energy to the sender.
  • Warns against hypnotism, auto-suggestion, and mental manipulation, advocating instead for the declaration of objective truth and the development of intuition (the sixth sense).

Lesson 7: spiritual power and the allegory of the dream

  • Describes the attainment of spiritual power through total alignment with divine love, resulting in spiritual illumination or ecstasy (Samadhi/Nirvana).
  • Identifies the pineal gland as the physical organ representing the superconsciousness and receiving spiritual vibrations.
  • Explains the evolutionary sequence of purification: baptism by water (physical/magnetic renewal), baptism by fire (mental purification through love), and baptism by spirit (divine illumination).
  • Concludes with "The Dream," an extended allegorical narrative depicting Lucifer's fall through pride, his conflict and ultimate redemption through Christ's love, and the realization that cosmic suffering is an illusion.
