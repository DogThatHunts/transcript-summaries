The 2 Week Glycine Experiment that Recovers Liver Health

A recent human study found that just two weeks of glycine supplementation significantly improved liver health markers, independent of fat loss. The improvement was confirmed statistically, with liver health scores dropping after supplementation.

  • Glycine levels are reduced in people with fatty liver disease, and this deficiency may be linked to impaired metabolic pathways.
  • In animal studies, knocking out the AGXT1 enzyme (which replenishes glycine from glyoxylate) worsens liver disease, suggesting a direct biological role.
  • A two-week human trial showed measurable improvements in liver health, using a high dose of 100 mg/kg body weight.
  • The study had no control group and only included obese participants, so results may not generalize to all populations.
  • The findings are preliminary and speculative; more research is needed to confirm glycine’s role in human liver health.
