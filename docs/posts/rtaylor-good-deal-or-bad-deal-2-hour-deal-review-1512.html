Key insights

  • Strategic positioning for wholesalers: Wholesalers should avoid using terms like "acquisitions manager" or explicitly identifying as wholesalers to agents. Instead, they should speak confidently as the buyer and refer to "my lender" or "my contractor" to build immediate credibility.
