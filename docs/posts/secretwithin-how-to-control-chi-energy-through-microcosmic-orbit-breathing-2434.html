Guided practice: feeling energy in the hands

Before the main microcosmic orbit practice, SecretWithin offers a preliminary exercise to feel chi directly. At the center of each palm is an energetic opening called laogong, which functions as entry and exit doors for qi. The practice involves:

  1. Sitting or standing with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.
  2. Keeping palms together for a few seconds until warmth is noticed.
  3. Placing them in front of the chest, palms facing each other.
  4. Inhaling through the nose while slowly separating the hands; exhaling while bringing them together.
  5. Keeping attention on the space between the palms.
  6. Repeating several times, then leaving hands 5–10 centimeters apart and moving them gently as if shaping an invisible sphere.

SecretWithin instructs practitioners to notice warmth, tingling, or slight pressure—this is qi flowing through the hands. With consistent practice, sensitivity grows and the flow of qi becomes increasingly clear and tangible.

The microcosmic orbit: main practice

  1. Setup: Sit comfortably with back straight and eyes closed. Breathe deeply, relaxing shoulders and body. Gently place the tongue on the upper palate, closing the circuit between the ren mai (conception vessel) and du mai (governing vessel).

  2. Lower dan tian activation: Bring attention to the lower dan tian below the navel. Visualize a warm golden sphere there—the reserve of qi. With each inhalation, feel it grow, absorbing energy from the earth. With each exhalation, relax and distribute vitality throughout the body.

  3. Upward flow: Guide qi upward along the back, following the du mai channel to the crown of the head. Feel the ascending flow like a river of light.

  4. Downward flow: Exhale, allowing qi to descend along the front of the body through the ren mai channel, passing through the upper and middle dan tian, and returning to the lower dan tian.

  6. Closing: Gather qi in the lower dan tian. Exhale and gently press the abdomen with the hands to anchor the energy. Keep the tongue on the palate for a few more seconds, then slowly open the eyes, feeling renewed vitality.
