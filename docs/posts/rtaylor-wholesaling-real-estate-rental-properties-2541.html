Themes and advice

  1. Have a buyer first: RTaylor repeatedly emphasizes that finding buyers before deals is more efficient than the reverse.
  2. Slow and steady: Building habits over time (20 minutes per day) is more sustainable than burning out with 10-hour days.
  3. Verify claims: RTaylor provides addresses and title company names so skeptics can confirm deals independently.
  4. Location is irrelevant: Wholesaling can be done from anywhere (Egypt, Nigeria, Canada) via email and phone.
  5. Use AI for analysis: ChatGPT can review contracts, analyze inspection reports, and answer legal questions.
  6. Persistence pays: Following up with unresponsive realtors and sellers over days or weeks often yields results.
  7. Understand your market: Know common investment strategies (BRRRR, fix-and-flip, section-eight rentals) so you can speak intelligently with buyers.
