BPCA Board Meeting — 16 September 2026

The Battery Park City Authority board approved major South resiliency project closures and Northwest project updates, while addressing community concerns about environmental monitoring transparency.

Hugh L. Carey Battery Park City Authority · 52 min · 11,345-word transcript · source

Overview

The Hugh L. Carey Battery Park City Authority board met on September 16, 2026, to review organizational operations and major coastal resiliency projects. Key discussions included the near-completion of the South Battery Park City Resiliency Project (within 2.2% of budget), continued progress on the Northwest Resiliency Project, adoption of a strategic plan with six organizational goals, and robust community and cultural programming at Wagner Park. A public commenter raised significant concerns about delays in environmental monitoring reports during resiliency construction. The board approved several financial authorizations and contracts, and moved into executive session to discuss real estate negotiations.

    Raju announced the Authority's move to new offices on the 15th floor and introduced new staff members.   4. 8:58 Strategic Plan Implementation - Stacey

  5. 10:36 Arts and Culture Programming - Craig

    Craig reported on summer programming that attracted over 61,000 attendees and described the fall season, including the Bill T. Jones world premiere and upcoming events. The team has generated over 300,000 Instagram views through event coverage and is driving ticket signups.

  6. 17:30 Governance Committee Report - Goldie

    Goldie reported the governance committee reviewed and recommended distributing a revised conflicts and ethics policy and mission statement to the full board for comment, with votes to follow in October.

  7. 18:46 Audit Committee Report - Pam

    Pam reported the audit committee reviewed the April 2026 six-month financial statement and the audit plan for fiscal year-end 2026, which should be completed by January. Key issues reviewed included Brookfield lease extension, 2025 bond issuance, and a 2026 revolver.

  8. 20:03 MWBE Accomplishments - Jamalia

    Jamalia reported 15% MWBE utilization ($8 million of $55 million) for Q1 FY2027. For resiliency projects to date, $60 million (25%) has been paid to MWBEs on South Battery Park and $35 million (10%) on North Battery Park.

  9. 22:27 South Resiliency Project Update - Angela

    Angela reported the South Battery Park City Resiliency Project is nearly substantially complete, with the PRA opening and bikeways opened. The project is running approximately $6.2 million over budget (2.2%) on a $296 million contract. Plantings have been completed and remaining work includes finishing two sliver areas and testing flood barriers.

  10. 28:19 Northwest Resiliency Project Update - Dan and Rami

    Dan provided detailed update on Northwest Project progress across multiple reaches. Reach 7 (South Cove) is furthest along with sheet pile installation complete. North Cove represents the project bottleneck with complex engineering work ongoing. Rami reported on community engagement including new building-specific notification system with over 350 signups.

  11. 39:38 Corporate Actions - Stacey Flanagan Banking Authorization

    The board approved authorization for Chief Operating Officer Stacey Flanagan to sign financial and bank-related documentation for the Authority.

  12. 40:11 South Battery Park Construction Amendment - Angela

    Angela presented request to increase work order allowance for Battery Park Construction LLC Package 4 by $3.6 million, bringing the contract from $81.593 million to $85.193 million, driven primarily by underground work and delay impact costs.

  13. 41:59 FY2027 Budget and Financial Plan - Pam

    Pam presented the preliminary FY2027 budget and financial plan showing projected bond issuances of $400 million over three years, with $250 million projected for 2027. New bond issuance is targeted for June 2027.

  14. 45:50 Staffing Services Prequalified Panel - Stacey

    Stacey requested approval to establish two prequalified staffing panels: eight firms for permanent staffing and five for temporary staffing, with terms of three years plus two-year options, not to exceed $500,000 aggregate, including MWBE and WBE certified firms.

  15. 48:17 Consent Agenda

    The board approved four procurement items: WJE Engineers extension for site leak investigation ($40,000 increase to $60,000 total), In Our Shoes executive coaching amendment (increase to $280,000), Dragonetti Brothers Landscaping tree care services ($250,000), and Integrity General Contractors waste hauling services ($150,000).

  16. 51:43 Executive Session

    The board entered executive session to discuss negotiations related to lease of real property, the publicity of which could substantially affect property values.
