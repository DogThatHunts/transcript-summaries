Dave Champion on the Federal Income Tax — A Synthesis of 45 Video Transcripts

Where 45 videos reinforce each other — and where they contradict themselves.

A cross-reading of all 45 tax videos in the income-tax index — the original 33 (rows 1217–1255) plus 12 added 2026-11-13 (rows 5596–5612) — built to surface where Champion's arguments reinforce each other and where they conflict. Every finding is tagged with the video(s) it draws from and a confidence label. Analysis is grounded in the transcripts themselves, not the summaries.

Confidence: HIGH = same statute/claim stated two opposing ways, quotable both sides · MEDIUM = real tension, partly reconcilable as emphasis/context · LOW = minor or likely wording/ASR artifact. Where a claim conflicts with an external authority he cites (not with himself), it is labelled [claim vs. source].

Fidelity caveat: the analysis below is grounded in raw ASR transcripts (unreliable punctuation, occasional mis-hearings — e.g. "Brushaber" rendered as "Bruce Schaber"/"Bruce Schaefer"). A flagged contradiction could be substantive or an artifact of ASR/summary; it is labelled accordingly.

Citation policy for this page only: every statute, case, form, Treasury Decision, Act, and named report/book is linked on every occurrence, not just the first — an explicit, scoped exception to the site-wide "first mention only" rule that still applies to every other published summary (see publisher/CITATION_LINKING.md). Links are to the shared citation register where an entry exists, or the sibling transcription-agent/publisher/citations.py curated map, or a freshly verified source — never invented.

The central thesis (what every video is arguing)

One claim underlies all 45: the federal income tax, as actually written, was never imposed on ordinary Americans earning domestic income in the fifty states — they pay only out of ignorance or fear induced by a decades-long government "disinformation campaign." What differs video-to-video is the route he takes to that conclusion, and those routes are the source of most of the inconsistencies below.

He builds the thesis on four sub-argument pillars, used in varying combination:

  • P1 Definitional — statutory terms of art ("employee", "wages", "includes", "U.S. person", "trade or business", "state") are narrow and exclude ordinary Americans.
  • P2 Forms/withholding — W-4/W-9/1040/1099 apply only to the federally-connected; signing them self-inflicts a false legal presumption.
  • P3 Constitutional — the income tax is an excise on a privilege; labor and property are rights and cannot be taxed directly without apportionment.
  • P4 Jurisdiction/enforcement — "state"/"United States" mean D.C./territories; the IRS enforces by bluff, hidden Treasury Decisions, and computer presumptions.

The 12 new videos add no fifth pillar — every claim in them slots into one of the same four — but they supply the clearest statement yet of the mechanics behind P1/P2: the "withholding agent" chain (rows 5596, 5599, 5600, 5602, 5608, 5610, 5612) and, for the first time across the whole corpus, the complete numbered list of the "Treasury Decisions restricting Form 1040" claim (row 5612 — see Reinforcement, below).

Where the argument reinforces itself (high agreement across videos)

These claims recur near-verbatim across many videos and form the load-bearing spine:

  • Form W-9 is a "withholding certificate" whose sole purpose is to certify, under penalty of perjury, that you hold a foreign person's U.S.-source income as their domestic agent. The single most-repeated claim in the corpus, restated with near-identical statutory support (26 CFR §1.1471-1(b)(148), 26 CFR §1.1441-1(c)(16)) in the new batch. (see: 1217, 1219, 1220, 1225, 1226, 1232, 1236, 1239, 1241, 1243, 1247, 1249, 1250, 5596, 5598, 5600, 5602, 5606, 5608, 5610, 5612)
  • Form 1040 is only for non-resident aliens / foreign corporations or their domestic agents, per a run of Treasury Decisions since 1913 — none of which names an ordinary American. (see: 1217, 1218, 1221, 1226, 1227, 1236, 1237, 1239, 1245, 1247, 1249, 1252, 1254, 5596, 5599, 5603, 5605, 5608, 5610, 5612) — **row 5612 is the one place in the entire corpus that names the full list: T.D. 1928, T.D. 1953, T.D. 2013, T.D. 2190, T.D. 2313, T.D. 2401, T.D. 2402, T.D. 2815, T.D. 2988, T.D. 6500 — every other video that makes this claim (including row 1217 in the original batch) gives only a count.
  • The statutory "employee" (§3401(c)) means only government officers/employees — private-sector workers cannot be read in — via the "includes/means" term-of-art rule. (see: 1219, 1222, 1231, 1234, 1241, 1244, 1246, 1254, 5599, 5605, 5606, 5608, 5610)
  • Signing a federal tax form is the self-inflicted act that creates liability — the presumption is manufactured by your own signature, not imposed by statute. (see: 1217, 1218, 1223, 1227, 1232, 1236, 1237, 1243, 1255, 5596, 5598, 5599, 5600, 5602, 5606)
  • The income tax is a constitutional excise on a government-granted privilege; earning a living and owning property are rights, so they can't be taxed directly. (see: 1218, 1224, 1235, 1236, 1249, 1252, 1253, 5599, 5606)
  • The income tax exists to pay interest on the national debt to the Federal Reserve, not to fund services — corroborated by the Grace Commission. New to the corpus as of this batch, but stated with unusual confidence and now repeated three times: the Grace Commission (Reagan-era) found "100% of the income tax... service[s] interest on the national debt... not one penny" funds public services, and the income tax and the Federal Reserve Act were passed "the same year, same congressional session" in 1913. (see: 5596, 5605, 5606)
  • Remedy: read the book first, then rebut every form and build an evidentiary record — never "go off half-cocked." His own book, Income Tax: Shattering the Myths, is invoked as the authoritative source almost every video in both batches, frequently under an ASR-garbled title ("Shattering the Mist"/"the Mess"/"the Miss"). (see: 1221, 1223, 1226, 1231, 1235, 1237, 1239, 1243, 1249, 1250, 1252, 1255, 5596, 5597, 5598, 5599, 5600, 5602, 5603, 5605, 5606, 5608, 5610, 5612)
  • Personal proof-of-concept: he has not filed or paid in ~30–32 years (since the early 1990s). (see: 1223, 1225, 1233, 1250, 1253, 5596, 5603, 5606)
  • The "three classes of persons" framework (non-resident alien w/ U.S.-source income; foreign corporation w/ U.S.-source income; U.S. citizen abroad w/ foreign-earned income) is restated verbatim, with no variation, across every video in the new batch — it is Champion's single foundational premise, underlying every P2/P3/P4 claim. A fourth, non-taxed "withholding agent" role (anyone who merely handles one of the three classes' income) recurs in 5599, 5600, 5602, 5608, 5610, 5612 as the mechanism connecting ordinary Americans' forms/withholding to the theory without making them personally liable.

Structural note: reinforcement here is largely by restatement — the videos share a fixed opening thesis and biographical hook. He also presents his two independent derivations (P1 definitional and P3 constitutional-excise) as mutually confirming, though they are logically distinct paths to the same endpoint.

Inconsistencies & contradictions

1. The withholding class is defined two incompatible ways. HIGH The same §3402 / Chapter 24 wage-withholding regime is said to reach "only government employees" (via §3401(c)) in some videos and "only non-resident aliens with U.S.-source wages" in others. Row 1246 and row 1247 make the opposing claims about the identical statute. The new batch adds further weight on the NRA side without ever reconciling it with the government-employee reading from rows like 1219/1222/1246: row 5608 argues §3402 targets NRAs employed by government entities — an attempt to merge both readings that isn't picked up by any other video, including 5610/5612, which argue §3402/T.D. 8734 reach NRA wage income generally, with no government-employment qualifier. (govt-employee: 1219, 1222, 1231, 1234, 1241, 1244, 1246, 1254 — vs — NRA/foreign-person: 1217, 1236, 1237, 1239, 1247, 1249, 1252, 5610, 5612 — vs — hybrid NRA-government-employee: 5608)

2. "Only two classes — no third class," then a third (and fourth, and fifth) class. MEDIUM-HIGH Rows 1236 and 1239 assert flatly that the tax reaches "only two classes… no third class," then in the next breath describe the U.S.-person/withholding-agent as a third role. The new batch makes this worse, not better: every one of 5599/5600/5602/5608/5610/5612 explicitly adds a fourth "withholding agent" class (not taxed itself, but liable to collect/remit someone else's tax) on top of the three — so the corpus now contains "two," "three," and "three-plus-a-fourth-liability-class" framings of the same underlying scheme, none of which is flagged by Champion as a revision of an earlier count. (see: 1236, 1239 vs. 1221, 1226, 1227, 1249 vs. 5599, 5600, 5602, 5608, 5610, 5612)

3. The Treasury-Decision count drifts — and is still never fully itemized except once. MEDIUM The number of TDs supposedly restricting Form 1040 to non-resident aliens climbs: 7 (1217) → 9 (1218) → "9 or 10" (1219, 1227) → 11 (1236, 1237, 1239, 1245, 1247, 1252, 1254). Row 1249 partly resolves this ("8 when I wrote the book, now 11"). The new batch repeats the same pattern of asserting a count without naming numbers — row 5596 says "8... now 10," row 5599/5605/5608/5610 all say "10" or "no less than 10" — except row 5612, which is the only video in all 45 to actually name the ten: 1928, 1953, 2013, 2190, 2313, 2401, 2402, 2815, 2988, 6500 — ten numbers for a claim previously made, bare, 17+ times across both batches without one of them ever being named. Low-stakes on its own, yet it undercuts the "100% consistent, I've read every one" framing that recurs in both batches.

4. "includes" is applied in whichever direction the conclusion needs. MEDIUM He defines "includes" as a narrowing "term of limited expansion" (1219, 1222, 1231, 1234, 5599, 5600, 5602, 5608, 5610) — illustrated in the new batch with a repeated fruit/food analogy ("includes" creates a closed category, like "fruit" includes strawberries but not steak) — but in 1244 uses the same word expansively to sweep all branches of government into §3401(c) "employee." The interpretive engine bends to the required result. (see: 1231, 1244)

5. Enforcement is "pure bluster" — except when it isn't. MEDIUM Row 1223 frames IRS enforcement as intimidation that "evaporates when you know the law," yet the same video (and 1249) warns "don't ever, ever try this without study," notes he was "one of the 10 most wanted," and concedes people who stop filing get IRS letters for prior years. The new batch adds a parallel asymmetry: row 5599/5606 describe a federal civil injunction action against Champion personally (alleging he made three specific frivolous claims he denies making, with a disputed pseudonymous government witness), while row 5596/5603/5606 separately insist he's never been criminally prosecuted because the government "can't afford to take that loss" — so the corpus holds both "they've already sued me" and "they're too afraid to ever touch me" without reconciling the two. (see: 1223, 1249, 1235, 1245, 1219, 1239, 5596, 5599, 5603, 5606)

6. He concedes the classic protester arguments are false — while landing on the same bottom line. MEDIUM Rows 1230 and 1249 explicitly agree with the courts that "person excludes natural persons," "wages aren't taxable," and "filing is voluntary" are false/frivolous — distancing himself from Schiff, the §861 position (Snipes), and Tedder. Row 5606 restates this almost verbatim as the three specific claims the government's civil injunction accused him of making — which he denies making, while his own conclusion ("no law requires you to pay one red cent") is the same endpoint those "false" arguments reach; the distinction rests entirely on his form-signing theory. (see: 1230, 1249, 1227, 5606)

7. Moore v. United States is read against itself — and a second, unnamed case joins it in the new batch. MEDIUM-HIGH claim vs. source He cites Moore (2024) as confirming the income tax is an avoidable excise and that ordinary Americans owe nothing (1243, 1249, 1252), quoting the syllabus line "taxes on income are indirect taxes." But Moore actually upheld the tax against the taxpayers, and row 1252 mis-names it "Moore v. Hooks v. United States." Row 5605 appears to reference the same Moore unrealized-gains controversy without naming it ("current/pending Supreme Court case on taxing unrealized gains"), predicting it "will be struck down" — a prediction Moore itself had already resolved the opposite way by the time most of this batch was recorded, suggesting either a stale take or a different, unidentified case. (see: 1252, 1249, 1243, 5605)

8. Sales tax: doesn't apply to him, yet he pays it. LOW-MEDIUM Row 1233 argues sales tax can't touch his selling activity, but his own worked pricing example has him paying sales tax on wholesale purchases — implicitly conceding that transaction is taxable. His sole-proprietor "it's a right" logic is also never reconciled with his stated basis for the tax (a foreign/out-of-state corporation's "doing-business" privilege), which would still reach in-state incorporated small businesses. (see: 1233)

9. Social Security: "just as much a scam" but reached by two different, now three, routes. LOW-MEDIUM Row 1253 equates FICA with the income tax ("just as much a scam," quoting Stewart Machine calling it "merely an additional income tax"), arguing it via a jurisdictional route. Row 5606 reaches the same "FICA isn't really Social Security" conclusion by a third, different route — an unnamed Supreme Court holding that FICA is "just an additional income tax" into the general fund, with Social Security law existing mainly for reciprocal obligations to foreign nationals working in the U.S. — a new mechanism that doesn't cite or reconcile with 1253's jurisdictional/Stewart Machine argument for the identical conclusion. (see: 1253, 5606)

10. "No law exists" vs. "no one has found it in 112 years." LOW Row 1246 refuses to assert the authorizing withholding statute doesn't exist (only that no one has located it) — then states flatly "there is no law." The new batch repeats the identical epistemic slide: row 5598 concedes "one does not preclude the other" (a domestic-only filing requirement could theoretically exist) then treats 111 years of not finding one as conclusive proof none exists; row 5610 bases the "payor = withholding agent, no exceptions" reading purely on his own 31 years of personal reading, not a demonstrated negative. (see: 1246, 5598, 5610)

11. The "no-implication" interpretive rule is not applied to his own inferences. MEDIUM new to this batch Row 5602 invokes Gould v. Gould — tax statutes must be construed narrowly against the government, never extended "by implication beyond the clear import of the language used" — to argue §1 and its regulation, 26 CFR §1.1-1, are too "skeletal" to tax anyone by implication. He then reaches his own affirmative three-classes conclusion by inference from Chapter 3's cross-referenced source-income limits — a limitation §1 itself never states — applying by implication exactly the kind of extension Gould forbids, just in his own favor rather than the government's.

12. "Zero forms required of ordinary Americans" vs. his own scenario, where ordinary Americans file forms. MEDIUM new to this batch Row 5600's stated conclusion is absolute: "in all of U.S. income tax law, zero forms are required of a U.S. citizen... None. Zip. Nada." His own worked example in the same video — an investment firm and a law firm, both ordinary domestic entities — requires them to furnish W-9s, withhold, and file precisely because they're "in the pipeline" handling someone else's foreign-connected income. He never cleanly separates "no forms are imposed on your own domestic income" from "forms are still required of you as an intermediary," and the absolute "zero" framing papers over that distinction.

13. One self-corrected arithmetic slip, one real cross-video date drift. LOW Row 5608 says a withholding-authority search has run "since 1942... 32 years have passed" (→ pointing to roughly 1974), in the same video that otherwise uses "111 years since 1913" for the identical Form-1040/TD timeline — the two counts don't resolve to the same baseline and the discrepancy is never addressed. Separately, row 5612 says "110 years" where rows 5608 and 5610 (recorded the same week, on the same 1913 baseline) say "111" — trivial on its own, but repeats the project's broader observation that Champion's elapsed-time figures drift slightly from video to video even when the underlying date (1913) is fixed.

The pattern underneath

The corpus is internally consistent on its conclusions and its most-cited artifacts (W-9, Form 1040, §3401(c), the Treasury Decisions) but inconsistent on the mechanism — it reaches "you owe nothing" by at least four distinct routes (foreign-person, government-employee, territorial-jurisdiction, constitutional-excise) that are presented interchangeably and, in the §3402 case, are mutually exclusive. The 12 new videos sharpen this pattern rather than resolving it: they add a fourth "withholding agent" liability class that none of the original 33 counted when asserting "only two" or "only three" classes, and they finally supply the one piece of hard data (row 5612's ten named Treasury Decisions) that the rest of the corpus had spent 44 videos citing only as a bare count. Certainty tracks argument type: definitional/statutory claims are absolute; enforcement, motive, and technology claims are openly speculative (now joined by the Grace Commission/Federal Reserve Act "why" narrative, asserted with equal confidence but resting on a commission report and a shared enactment year, not statutory text); and the affirmative "how-to" is almost always deferred to the paid book, Income Tax: Shattering the Myths. The strongest single thread — repeated in a dozen-plus videos and never contradicted — is the narrow, specific claim that the W-9 is a withholding certificate about a foreign person's money; the widest fault line, now wider than before, is how many classes of person the scheme actually has, and who the fourth, non-taxed "withholding agent" role really is.
